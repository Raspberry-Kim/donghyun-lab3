DATASET:
Contains the Haslemere dataset released by Stephen M. Kissler, Petra Klepac,
Maria Tang, Andrew J. K. Conlan and Julia R. Gog with the manuscript
'Sparking "The BBC Four Pandemic": Leveraging citizen science and mobile
phones to model the spread of disease' (bioRxiv 479154, 2018),
https://github.com/skissler/haslemere (commit dc49e83).
The upstream README states GPL-3 for its code and gives no separate license
for the data files; the dataset is described as publicly released in the
manuscript and reused by later studies. The files Kissler_DataS1.csv and
Kissler_DataS2.csv are included unchanged in data/raw/, and data/village.json
contains a lossless re-encoding of the same values. No additional license is
claimed over the dataset. Preserve the attribution to the original authors
and repository when reusing it. See CREDITS.md and PROVENANCE.md.
